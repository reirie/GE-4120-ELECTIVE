For this project, I focused on addressing poor sleep habits among adults and showing how technology can help promote healthier sleep routines. Sleep is so crucial for brain health, memory, and energy, yet over 30% of adults struggle to get the recommended 7 to 9 hours a night due to work stress, late-night screen time, caffeine, and erratic schedules. To tackle this, my report introduces **SleepSmart**, a concept for a mobile app designed to track sleep behaviors, send helpful reminders, and guide users toward better sleep hygiene.

For the infographic design, I went with a cozy night-sky background featuring dark blues, purples, soft lavender text boxes, and bright yellow stars to set a calming mood. I used bold, easy-to-read headings and split the content into quick, digestible chunks like pill-shaped tip boxes and side-by-side comparisons of causes and effects. To make it relatable for social media, I also included relatable illustrations of someone struggling to sleep versus resting peacefully.

My creative process started with diving into CDC and NHLBI research on sleep deprivation to build a strong foundation. From there, I mapped out SleepSmart's features and translated the heavy medical information into a simple visual storyline—moving from the main problem to its causes, health impacts, and practical tech-driven solutions. Finally, I put everything together in a clean, visual layout meant to quickly catch people's attention on their social feeds.
