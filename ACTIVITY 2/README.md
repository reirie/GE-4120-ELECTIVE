For my logo, I chose a moon and jewelry-inspired design with the name “Arcana Moonlit Jewelries.” I selected this concept because the moon gives the brand a magical, elegant, and dreamy feeling, which fits well with jewelry. The crescent moon, stars, and delicate details also make the logo feel more unique and feminine while showing the idea of beauty and mystery.

For the colors, I used Shadow Iris, Lavender, Light Yellow, English Rose, and Light Lavender. The dark purple gives the design a classy and elegant base, while the lavender and pink add a soft and delicate feel. The light yellow gives a slight warm and luxurious touch, making the overall palette feel more magical and inviting. I chose these colors because they match the moonlit and jewelry theme while keeping the brand visually soft and memorable.

For typography, I used Abril Fatface as the display font because its bold and elegant appearance makes the brand name stand out. Agrandin is used for body text because it is clean and easy to read, while Fredoka adds a more playful and friendly touch for smaller details and accents. Together, these fonts create a balance between elegance, readability, and personality, helping the overall brand look polished but still approachable.
