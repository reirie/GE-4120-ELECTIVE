I used different design principles to make my “FREE COFFEE!” poster look eye-catching and easy to understand. Emphasis is shown through the big and bold “FREE COFFEE!” text, which is the first thing people notice. Contrast is used by combining the dark brown and black elements with the light background, making the important text easier to see. Hierarchy also helps by putting the main message first, followed by the tagline, date, and location, so the information can be understood quickly.

Balance is created by placing the large coffee cup on the lower-right side to balance the text on the left. Repetition and consistency can be seen in the brown, cream, and black colors and the repeated rounded shapes, which make the poster look more connected. Alignment and proximity keep the information organized by placing related details close together. Overall, these design principles helped make the poster more attractive, organized, and easy to read, while still matching the fun and cozy theme of the coffee event.
